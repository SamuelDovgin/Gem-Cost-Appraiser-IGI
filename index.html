Seller & market modifiers
Chinese factory direct −20–30% cost
Henan / Wuzhou HPHT/CVD factory selling on TikTok Shop or Alibaba. Eliminates Indian cutter, US importer. China = 40–50% global production at ~$0.04/kWh. Adds TikTok fee ~5%.
High-volume auction seller −10–20% ask
Jeweler running 50+ lots/day (eBay, Worthy, I Do Now I Don't, etc.) operates on thin margin, fast turns. Lower overhead, lower hold cost, needs volume throughput.
IGI Triple Excellent (3EX) +5–8% ws
Excellent cut + excellent polish + excellent symmetry. The gold standard for lab-grown. Adds measurable premium over standard Ideal at wholesale.
HPHT grown (not CVD) +5–12% ws
HPHT avoids CVD's striation/haziness issue; "as-grown" HPHT stones are cleaner. Wholesale commands slight premium, especially for D–F colors. (Most Chinese factory stones are HPHT.)
As-grown (no post-treatment) +3–8% ws
CVD stones often need HPHT post-treatment to fix color. An untreated "as-grown" stone is noted on the cert and commands a premium from buyers who care.
GIA certified (not IGI) +8–15% retail
GIA no longer grades lab-grown 4Cs (as of 2023) but still issues origin reports. IGI is the standard for lab-grown. A GIA cert on a lab stone is unusual and adds resale comfort.

    White lab audit outcome
    The wholesale floor remains very low; online retail is now often only ~2–3× trade, not old 5–10× spreads.
    D–F / VVS premiums are compressed; cut, growth notes, and video matter more than tiny clarity jumps.
    Large white stones are cheap online, so the model treats traditional retail as an ask ceiling, not a target.
    Chinese/direct and high-volume auction toggles should be used for real deal checks.

    Fancy color audit outcome
    Fancy color wholesale is thin and inconsistent; direct-sale comps are more useful than old natural-color analogies.
    Yellow and brown/black sit closest to white-lab pricing; vivid pink/blue/red keep the strongest premiums.
    Green/orange/purple premiums are modeled between yellow and vivid pink/blue due to lower demand depth.
    Intensity drives more value than VVS vs VS in lab fancy colors.
    Fancy vivid pink comps
    Friendly Diamonds lists a 5.00ct Fancy Vivid Pink VS1 marquise at $2,898 sale / $4,830 crossed-out retail.
    Recent 5ct Fancy Vivid Pink VS1 marquise auction close: $1,585.
    This pins the model's 5ct vivid-pink auction/direct fair zone to low-four-figures, not legacy $5k–$10k retail.
    Above that, beauty/video can justify a premium, but it is no longer automatic scarcity pricing.
    Ratio / shape demand
    Current buyer preference favors balanced rounds/squares and elegant elongated ovals, pears, radiants, emeralds, and marquises.
    Ratio does not grade light performance, but it affects face-up desirability and resale friction.
    Out-of-range ratios should be discounted unless color saturation, spread, and video are clearly strong.
    The ratio badge is advisory; measurements and actual video still win.
    Pricing model methodology
    White baseline (v3.1): Rebased to E/VS1/Round/Ideal — the Alibaba commodity floor (most IGI listings are DEF + VVS/VS). 1ct E/VS1 ≈ $100 trade cost, calibrated to direct-sale signal IGI LG563297279 and Messi 1ct D VS1 ($121.50 factory → E-adjusted). Wuzhou Messi D-color selector is converted to your color grade for the ceiling row.
    Fancy color baseline: Rebased to 2026 direct/auction lab-grown signals across yellow, pink, blue, green, orange, purple, brown, black, and red. Fancy Vivid Pink was specifically pinned to 5ct marquise comps around $1.6k auction and ~$2.9k direct-sale retail. Clarity matters less for fancy color — saturation and seller channel drive most of the spread.
    Magic-weight continuity: The model applies a smooth approach-discount for stones within 0.15–0.22ct below a major carat threshold (1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5ct). A 2.9ct stone does not command the "3-carat diamond" demand premium — it is discounted relative to a 3.0ct stone. A 3.09ct stone fully captures the 3ct tier. The discount tapers to zero exactly at each threshold (no hard price step).
    Color grade mult (white only): vs E = 1.00. D +8%, F −8%, G −12%, H −20%, I −28%, J −37%, steeper below J. Grades worse than E are off-catalog on most Alibaba IGI white listings.
    Clarity mult (carat-dependent): Table 5B (v3.1). At 1ct: SI1 −32%, SI2 −42%. At 2ct: SI1 −52%, SI2 −62% (LG563297279 anchor). At 3ct: VVS1 +36%, SI2 −60%. Below VS1 is off-catalog on Alibaba. Fancy color clarity stays compressed (~50%).
    Cert-derived modifiers (auto from IGI PDF): CVD (not as-grown) −10% ws. Post-growth treatment −6%. No cut grade on cert −12% (polish/sym only). Pear/oval/marquise/emerald + SI1/SI2 −10%. 3EX premium only applies when cut grade is Excellent on the cert.
    Shape mult: Round baseline 1.0. Oval/pear +5–8% (demand premium). Moval (marquise–oval hybrid) ~−6% vs round — trendier than marquise but still non-standard on IGI. Dutch / hexagonal Dutch ~−18% (custom geometric marquise; Rapaport: marquise already among the deepest fancy discounts). Portuguese ~−15% (deep extra-facet cut; smaller face-up per carat). Flanders / generic hexagonal ~−17–21%. Rose / briolette −28–30%.
    Toggle modifiers: Chinese factory: −22% wholesale, fair price 1.3–1.6× ws. High-volume auction seller: fair price 1.2–1.45× ws (thin margins, fast turns). 3EX: +6% ws. HPHT: +8% ws. As-grown: +5% ws. GIA cert: +12% retail only.
    Fair price markup: Standard seller 1.55–2.05× ws (covers IGI cert $80–150, shipping, platform fees, returns buffer, thin margin). Auction jeweler running volume 1.2–1.45× ws. Chinese factory 1.3–1.6× ws. Traditional retail 3.8–7× ws (per Tenoris 74% gross margin data).
