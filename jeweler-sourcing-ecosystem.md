# Lab-Grown Diamond Sourcing Ecosystem — Jeweler Perspective

**Research Date:** May 19, 2026  
**Scope:** Where jewelers actually source IGI-certified lab-grown white diamonds, how prices compare across channels, and Alibaba fancy-cut pricing data.

---

## The Big Picture

Lab-grown prices dropped **~74% from 2020 to early 2025** (Wikipedia / Guardian, Jan 2025) and continue falling as CVD grow time collapsed from weeks → hours. That deflation is baked into every channel below.

The channel you source from determines your price floor. The gap between the cheapest source (Alibaba factory direct) and the most expensive (US wholesale importer) is **15–40%** on the same stone.

---

## Sourcing Channels — Ordered Cheapest → Most Expensive

### Tier 1 — Factory Direct (Absolute Floor)

**Who:** Wuzhou, Guangdong, and Surat factories listing directly on B2B platforms.  
**MOQ:** 1 stone. No membership fees.  
**Barrier:** None except payment risk and patience.

#### 1A. Alibaba.com
- The most accessible factory-direct channel globally.
- IGI-certified sellers exist but are rare — only a handful have actual IGI stock in-house. Most will order from the lab post-purchase (adds 1–2 weeks).
- **Only verified IGI seller found with live D/Round price ladder:** Wuzhou Messi Gems (`/product-detail/...1600612782670`). Money-back guarantee + 3-day dispatch = they hold stock.
- Other verified sellers for loose IGI: YINZHENG (oval/round 1–5ct), Mishang (pear/round), Zhanhao Jewelry (oval).
- Non-IGI sellers are very common and price 20–40% below IGI. Always confirm the actual certificate on order.
- **Payment risk:** Alibaba Trade Assurance reduces it significantly; use only Trade Assurance orders.
- **Shipping:** DHL/FedEx from Shenzhen/Wuzhou, ~3–5 business days to US.

#### 1B. Made-in-China.com
- Similar to Alibaba, slightly smaller selection for gems, less vetting.
- IGI-certified listings harder to verify.

#### 1C. 1688.com (Chinese domestic)
- Alibaba's domestic Chinese platform — prices 10–20% below Alibaba international.
- All listings in Chinese. Requires Chinese business registration to purchase directly. Some freight forwarders will buy on behalf.
- Not practical for most US/EU jewelers without an agent.

#### 1D. Direct WeChat/WhatsApp from Wuzhou factories
- After one Alibaba order, many factories will solicit direct WeChat orders at 5–10% below listed Alibaba price (cutting out Alibaba's fee).
- No buyer protection. Only recommended after establishing trust over several orders.

### Tier 2 — B2B Diamond Marketplaces (Verified Supply, Slightly Higher)

These platforms sit between factories and retailers. They add vetting, returns, and faster logistics but charge 10–20% above Alibaba.

#### 2A. Nivoda (nivoda.net / nivoda.com)
- **HQ:** London, UK. Founded 2017. Global reach.
- **What it is:** B2B diamond marketplace connecting jewelers directly to diamond suppliers (cutters, importers, other retailers reselling). Not a retailer itself.
- **Lab-grown coverage:** Extensive. Searchable by cut, carat, color, clarity, cert (IGI, GIA, etc.), price.
- **Access:** Free account to browse. Orders require business verification.
- **Key feature:** Integrates directly into Shopify stores via "Nivoda Connect" — jewelers can list Nivoda inventory on their website and drop-ship directly to customers.
- **Pricing:** ~10–20% above Alibaba factory; still below US importers.
- **Vetting:** Suppliers are verified. Returns possible within policy.
- **Best for:** EU/UK jewelers, or anyone who wants vetted supply without dealing with Alibaba directly.

#### 2B. RapNet (rapaport.com/rapnet)
- **Rapaport** publishes the benchmark "Rap Price" list for natural diamonds (paywalled, ~$300/yr).
- Lab-grown section launched ~2022. Prices listed as % off natural Rap price.
- **Access:** Requires Rapaport membership and trade verification.
- **Lab-grown prices on RapNet:** Typically 20–40% above Alibaba because sellers include US and EU importers with overhead.
- **Best for:** Large dealers, established jewelers who already subscribe.

#### 2C. IDEX Online (idexonline.com)
- Israeli-run global exchange. Similar to RapNet. Paywalled.
- Strong for natural diamonds; lab-grown section smaller but growing.
- **Access:** Trade verification required.

#### 2D. VDB — Virtual Diamond Boutique (vdb.com)
- US-focused B2B platform for retailers. Memo programs available.
- Integrates with retail management software (Edge, Podium, etc.).
- Lab-grown selection growing; mostly US importers so prices are higher than Alibaba.

#### 2E. Polygon (polygon.net)
- US jeweler trade network. Older platform. Message boards + listings.
- Members sell and buy from each other. Prices variable — can be at or above retail.
- Best for secondhand/estate; less useful for new lab-grown sourcing.

### Tier 3 — US / EU Wholesale Importers (Most Accessible, Highest Markup)

These companies import from India/China, add overhead, and resell to US/EU jewelers.  
Typical markup: **Alibaba + 20–35%** for equivalent spec.

#### 3A. Grown Diamond Corporation (GDC)
- US importer. Ships from India. Well-regarded in the trade.
- Sells IGI and GIA certified. D–F, VVS–VS common stock.
- **Pricing example:** 1ct D/VVS1 Round ≈ $160–$190 (Alibaba: ~$125). That's ~28–52% premium.
- Account required; trade credentials checked.

#### 3B. Clean Origin
- B2C brand but has a **wholesale/trade program** for jewelers.
- Prices are between retail and Alibaba (not factory floor prices).
- Good selection, fast US shipping, return policy.

#### 3C. Lightbox Jewelry (De Beers subsidiary)
- **Fixed pricing:** $800/ct for standard colorless lab-grown. Does NOT vary by carat.
- **No IGI cert** on most stones (they have their own internal grading).
- Good for melee and fashion jewelry; not competitive for center stones vs Alibaba at 2ct+.
- Lab-grown prices from China made Lightbox's fixed pricing look expensive by 2024.

#### 3D. Stuller / Rio Grande / Hoover & Strong
- Traditional US jewelry supply houses. Added lab-grown in 2022–2023.
- Prices are 30–50% above Alibaba but include US-based customer service, returns, memo programs.
- Better for finished mountings; less competitive for loose stones.

---

### Tier 4 — India Direct

Surat, India is the world's #1 diamond cutting city. Most lab-grown roughs grown in China/India are cut in Surat.

#### 4A. Surat Manufacturers (Direct)
- Prices close to Alibaba factory level. Some even cheaper.
- WhatsApp/email negotiation. Payment via SWIFT wire.
- India sellers on Alibaba show DEF/VVS at 1ct for **$55–$58/stone** — these are likely uncertified or self-certified.
- IGI-certified Surat stones: slightly above Alibaba China prices but comparable.

#### 4B. Bharat Diamond Bourse (BDB) — Mumbai
- Physical exchange. Members only. Has lab-grown wing since ~2022.
- Prices at or near Surat factory level. Not practical for remote access.

## Where Jewelers Actually Buy (Reality Check)

| Jeweler Type | Primary Channel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Small independent | US importer (GDC, Clean Origin) OR Alibaba (adventurous) | Easy credit terms, English support |
| Mid-size retailer | Nivoda + local importer | Vetted supply, Shopify integration |
| Large chain / custom house | RapNet + direct India relationship | Volume discounts, memo programs |
| Savvy cost-conscious | Alibaba direct (Messi or YINZHENG) | Lowest price available anywhere |
| Online-first DTC brand | Nivoda or GDC | Drop-ship compatibility |

**The key takeaway:** A jeweler who buys from Alibaba is at the bottom of the cost curve. Most jewelers are paying 15–35% more than Alibaba prices. Your app's Alibaba comp is the absolute floor, not typical cost.

## Off-Catalog Grades — The Jeweler Secondary / Liquidation Market

**This is a distinct sourcing channel that does not appear in the Alibaba or importer tiers above.** Standard factory and importer stock covers DEF color + VVS/VS clarity. Outside that band — VS2 at odd sizes, G/H/I color, SI1, SI2, stones with treatment notes — you are not ordering from a catalog. You are buying from a jeweler who already owns a stone and needs to liquidate it.

### Why these stones exist as backstock

- Jeweler bought speculatively and the customer changed their mind
- Estate or pawnshop buy-in came as a package deal (had to take the whole lot)
- Memo return was rejected or went unsold
- Ordered for a custom piece that fell through
- Impulse buy at a trade show that didn't move at retail

The selling jeweler paid **US importer price** (20–35% above Alibaba). They need cash. They cannot return the stone. They will take a loss, but they don't always know how big a loss is fair — which creates a buying opportunity if you know the real market price.

### What qualifies as secondary-market / off-catalog

| Grade | Alibaba listed? | Primary channel |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| DEF / VVS1–VS1 / Round or popular fancy | ✓ Yes — standard catalog | Factory direct or importer |
| VS2 / DEF / Round | ✓ Yes — listed (Messi data shows it) | Factory or importer; secondary also available |
| VS2 / G–H color | ✗ No — off-DEF-catalog | **Jeweler secondary** |
| SI1 / any color | ✗ No — not stocked | **Jeweler secondary** |
| SI2 / any color | ✗ No — distressed lot | **Jeweler secondary** |
| Any clarity / I–Z color | ✗ No | **Jeweler secondary** |

### What to pay — relative to model wholesale price

The app's `ws` output for off-catalog grades reflects the jeweler secondary market **ceiling** — what a fair price looks like given the stone's grade. A motivated seller will often accept below model.

| Stone type | Target buy vs model ws | Notes |
|------------|------------------------|-------|
| VS2 (G–H color) | 0.75–0.90× model | Double-discounted stone; seller knows it's not DEF/VS1 |
| SI1 (any color) | 0.70–0.85× model | No factory comp exists; model is the ceiling, not the floor |
| SI2 (any color) | 0.60–0.80× model | Very distressed; few retail buyers; negotiate hard |
| G/H color + VS1–VS2 | 0.80–0.90× model | Off-DEF; seller may not know how much their color grade costs them |
| Any grade with treatment note | 0.75–0.90× model | CVD post-treatment or color treatment adds buyer skepticism |

### Where to find secondary/liquidation stock

- **Polygon.net** — US jeweler trade network with member-to-member listings
- **RapNet** "Trading" section — includes distressed and liquidation lots
- **Jewelers of America (JA) member network** — regional chapters, trade shows, WhatsApp groups
- **Facebook groups** — "Diamond Dealers" and regional jewelry trade groups
- **Local jeweler outreach** — simply asking; most jewelers don't advertise backstock
- **Estate dealers / pawnshop wholesale** — often have off-grade lab-grown from bulk acquisitions
- **Trade shows** — JCK Las Vegas, JA New York; floor deals with quick-sale pricing

### Buying strategy for secondary stock

1. Use the app's model price as your reference ceiling. Never open above it.
2. Point out the off-catalog status directly: *"This isn't a DEF/VS standard piece — there's no Alibaba comp for it."*
3. For SI1/SI2: target 15–25% below model. For lower color: 10–20% below model.
4. Request a loupe video or in-person inspection. Inclusions and color are harder to evaluate from cert alone at SI grades.
5. Verify IGI at igi.world before any payment — backstock sometimes carries certs from old grading standards, is mismatched to the stone, or is a different stone than advertised.
6. Factor in: you will also have to sell this at a discount. Build your exit into the buy price.

## Implications for the App

1. **Alibaba D/VS1/Round = absolute price floor.** The app's model is now calibrated to this floor (May 19 2026 update). Any jeweler paying more bought from a higher-tier source.

2. **Most jewelers pay 15–35% more than Alibaba.** The app could show this as a contextual note ("If sourced from US importer, cost was likely $X–$Y").

3. **Fancy cuts typically cost less than round (except oval at 1ct).** The current shape multipliers in the app are broadly correct.

4. **IGI certified is critical.** Uncertified stones are 20–40% cheaper on Alibaba but cannot be verified. If a customer's appraisal cert says IGI, the stone was definitely not from an uncertified Alibaba listing.

5. **India sourcing (Surat) is real.** $55–$58 DEF VVS pear at 1ct from Indian sellers exists. If the cert is IGI India (domestic), it's different from IGI International. Worth noting for appraisal context.
