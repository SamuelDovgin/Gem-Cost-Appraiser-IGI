Capture Alibaba listing
  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Safari: View → Show Favorites Bar; Chrome: Bookmarks → Show bookmarks bar).
  2. Drag the blue button above onto the bar.
  3. On any alibaba.com product or search page, click Capture Alibaba listing. A green toast appears and the row is on your clipboard.
  4. Come back here and paste once — it appends automatically.
